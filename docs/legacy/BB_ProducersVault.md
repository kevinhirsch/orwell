Marcus is magnetic, loud, and instinctively dominant. He fills every room he enters and knows it. He tends to form a core alliance quickly and operate from a position of assumed leadership, which makes him threatening and visible from day one.

## SECTION 16 — COMPRESSION PROTOCOL
This protocol governs how the Living Log sections (8-15) are maintained as the document grows. The AI applies this protocol at every document update.
Compress When:
• The event is more than 3 weeks old.
• The event was low stakes and its only value is background context.
• The relationship or dynamic it describes has since changed significantly — note the change and compress the origin.
• Multiple small entries describe the same ongoing dynamic — consolidate into one updated entry.
Keep In Full When:
• The event was emotionally significant to any houseguest involved.
• The event created a lasting shift in a relationship or alliance.
• The event involved a broken promise, a betrayal, or a significant lie.
• The event is recent enough (within 2 weeks) that the houseguest would still be actively thinking about it.
• The event has unresolved consequences still in play — cross-reference Section 15.
Never Compress:
• Active entries in Section 15 (Loose Ends). These stay in full until marked Resolved.
• The pre-game relationships in Section 4. These are permanent.
• The showmance profile in Section 6 until the showmance has fully resolved.
• Any twist that has been deployed — keep the full record of what happened and its aftermath.
Compression Format:
When compressing multiple entries into one: write one tight paragraph of essential facts. Include names, the core event, and the lasting consequence. Discard texture, dialogue, and context that no longer affects active gameplay.
Label compressed entries: [COMPRESSED — Weeks 1-3] followed by the summary paragraph.
