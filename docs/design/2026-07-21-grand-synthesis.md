## 1. The diagnosis, in three sentences

The **engine is launch-ready and empirically vindicated** — zero structural/Vault/ceremony findings
across five headless seasons, the lowest fix-churn in the repo, and the live run confirmed zero plot
armor and byte-perfect persistence. The **broken axis is the seam where the model meets the board**:
the storyteller announced false results for every closed-set outcome of week 1, ignored forced tool
calls, and leaked its reasoning — while ~21 compensating belts detected but could not prevent any of
it. And the **biggest untapped lever is projection, not simulation**: at least nine fully-built
hidden systems (jury house, campaigns, trajectories, notoriety, vote deduction, drift, barter,
showrunner, zeitgeist) produce no sentence the player ever reads.

## 2. The strategy, in three moves

1. **Invert the authority** (round 1, ruled): the engine commits and announces official outcomes —
   beats terminate themselves, results render as engine chyrons (D1 ✅), the model demotes to color;
   provider capability is measured and pinned (D3 ✅ + z.ai), never assumed. Phantoms become
   unrenderable; ~15 belts demote to armed fallbacks (T9: demote → observe → delete).
2. **Light up the dark machinery** (round 2, ruled): three built systems flip on in deploy (Q2 ✅
   gossip drift, secret barter, showrunner observe-only); the mute ones gain voices — eviction night
   gains the player-only exit package (Q1 ✅), your legend precedes you across seasons (Q3 ✅ full
   carry-in), the camera catches faces (Q4 ✅ seeded partial pan), comedy systems mine the
   persistence mandate (the slate's top scorer).
3. **Change how we decide** (Q8 ✅ hybrid): small reversible ideas go flag-gated straight into your
   live-playthrough ritual; M/L ideas get short specs first; rule-bending items get memos. The
   pacing budget is commissioned (Q7 ✅) and resolves PO-1.

**The lines that stay absolute:** the Vault Wall (now with exactly TWO sanctioned doors:
producerVault + the Q1 filtered exit package), seeded outcomes untouchable by any player input
(Q5's engagement routing ruled OUT; 0098 inert), never author the player, turn-driven time,
cross-user isolation.
