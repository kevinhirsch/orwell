# 0013 — The Diary Room

> **Status:** Draft. Two distinct things wear the "diary room" name, and the split is an
> **integrity boundary** — keep them apart.
> **Executable spec:** [`0013-diary-room.feature`](./0013-diary-room.feature)

## 1. Summary

- **Player Diary Room** — a **player-level, out-of-character** channel: a private, pressure-free
  space between the player and the producers for honest strategy and processing. Its content is
  the **player's own knowledge**, but it has **no in-game pathway to any NPC**. It may inform the
  engine's read of the player's strategy; it must **never** drive NPC behavior. Producers
  proactively invite the player in at dramatic moments.
- **NPC confessionals** — **Vault-only** content (off-screen; witness set excludes the player).
  Generated by the off-screen sim (0003); they inform NPC voice/behavior but the player **never**
  sees them (Vault Wall, 0001).

These are two different walls: **DR → NPC** (this feature) and **Vault → player** (0001).

## 2. Scope

**In:** the player DR channel (OOC, private, **no NPC pathway**); the public/private gap; the
producer-prompt hook; NPC confessionals as Vault content (never surfaced).

**Out:** the Vault-Wall mechanics themselves (**0001**); the conversation system (**0012**); the
relationship model (**decision 0002**).

## 3. Player Diary Room

- **Enter** by saying "I'm going to the DR" / "Diary Room", or via a **producer prompt**. The
  engine shifts to producer mode — warm, curious, pressure-free (not therapist, not game-show host).
- The player's DR content enters the **player's knowledge** (Journal-visible) but is flagged
  **no-NPC-pathway**: the engine's player-strategy read may consume it; **NPC-knowledge
  derivation excludes it entirely**.

## 4. The public/private gap (honored completely)

If the player tells houseguests one thing and the DR something different, the engine honors the
distinction: NPCs act on what the player **said publicly** (a witnessed event), **never** on the
DR. The DR only **deepens the engine's understanding** of the player's strategy; it changes no
NPC's behavior in a way the first-person fiction couldn't explain.

## 5. Producer-prompted sessions

The engine proactively invites the player to the DR at natural dramatic beats — after a major
conversation, before a key decision, after an eviction, when the player's position shifts. It
should feel like a producer gently pulling the player aside, **not** an interruption.

## 6. NPC confessionals (Vault-only)

NPC confessionals are recorded with a witness set that **excludes the player**, live in the
Vault, and **never** surface on any player-facing surface (0001). They are part of the off-screen
texture (0003) and may inform how an NPC is voiced — but the player only ever infers them through
behavior and gossip, never reads them.

## 7. Contracts (stack-agnostic)

```
recordDiaryRoom(content) -> adds to player KnowledgeState, tagged NO_NPC_PATHWAY
playerStrategyRead() -> may consume DR-tagged knowledge          # engine-internal, not NPC-facing
deriveNpcKnowledge(...) -> MUST exclude NO_NPC_PATHWAY content    # the DR -> NPC wall
producerPrompt(state) -> suggest a DR moment | none              # dramatic-beat hook
# NPC confessionals are VaultStore content (engine-only), recorded with witnessSet excluding the player
```

## 8. Test strategy

- DR content appears in the **player's** knowledge.
- **DR → no NPC:** across seeded play, **no NPC's `KnowledgeState`** ever gains a DR-tagged fact,
  and no NPC decision changes because of DR content (cross-checks the 0002 scenario).
- **Public/private gap:** the player says X publicly and Y in the DR; NPCs act on **X**, never **Y**.
- **NPC confessionals** are Vault-only and never surface (extends the 0001 sentinel test).
- **Producer prompts** fire at dramatic beats, not randomly / not every turn.

## 9. Definition of Done

- [ ] Player DR content is player knowledge and is provably **unreachable by any NPC**.
- [ ] The public/private gap holds: NPC behavior follows public speech, never the DR.
- [ ] NPC confessionals stay Vault-only (sentinel green); never on a player surface.
- [ ] Producer prompts are dramatic-beat–driven and unobtrusive.

## 10. Dependencies

scenario), 0012 (public speech vs the DR), 0003 (confessionals as off-screen content).
